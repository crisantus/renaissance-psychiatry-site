Two ways to be seen

Same evaluation, same provider continuity. Choose the format that fits your life.

In-person appointments

Evaluations, follow-ups and therapy in a quiet, accessible office designed to feel unlike a clinic.

  • Longer initial evaluations with unhurried conversation
  • Accessible entrance, seating and treatment rooms
  • Same provider across evaluation, medication and therapy
  • Evening appointments available

Telehealth appointments

Secure video visits from home, work or school, for patients located in the states where our providers are licensed.

  • Evaluations, medication follow-ups and psychotherapy by video
  • A private space and a reliable connection are all you need
  • You must be in a state where your provider is licensed
  • Some appointments may require an in-person visit
